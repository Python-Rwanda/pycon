PyCon Rwanda is a community conference intended for networking and collaboration in the developer community.
We value the participation of every member of the community and want everyone to have an enjoyable and
fulfilling experience. All attendees, speakers, sponsors, volunteers and organizers must follow this
Code of Conduct at the conference, at related social events and in our online spaces.

## Our commitment

We want PyCon Rwanda to be safe and welcoming for everyone, whatever their background, gender, gender identity,
age, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, appearance or level of experience. Beginners and experts
belong here equally.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and kind, in person and online.
- Be patient with people who are learning. Everyone started somewhere.
- Give feedback that is constructive and about the work, not the person.
- Make room for others to speak, in sessions and in conversation.
- Respect people's privacy. Ask before photographing someone or sharing their personal details.
- Look out for each other. If you see something wrong, tell an organizer.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation or discrimination of any kind.
- Offensive comments, slurs or imagery, in talks, slides, sponsor booths or online.
- Unwelcome physical contact or sexual attention.
- Stalking, following, or photographing or recording someone against their wishes.
- Sustained disruption of talks, sessions or other events.
- Advocating for or encouraging any of the above.

Sponsors are also bound by this Code of Conduct: booths, materials and staff must not use sexualized images,
activities or language.

## Reporting a problem

If something makes you or someone else feel unsafe or unwelcome, please tell a member of the organizing team
(they wear organizer badges) or a volunteer at the registration desk, or email the address at the bottom of this page.

Reports are handled in confidence by a small group of organizers. We will not share your identity without your
permission. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first: dial **112** in Rwanda.

## What happens next

Organizers will review every report and may take any action they consider appropriate, including a warning,
removing a talk or booth, or expelling someone from the conference without a refund. We will follow up with the
person who reported once the matter is resolved.
